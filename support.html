Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Email us at brady@goodlunch.com from the address associated with your account and we’ll delete your account and associated data within 7 days.

How do I report inappropriate content or a user?

Use the report option in the App, or email brady@goodlunch.com with a link or screenshot. We review reports within 24 hours.

Why does Waldo ask for location, photos, or camera access?

Location helps us show nearby places on the map. Photos and camera access are used only when you choose to add a photo or video to a place or post. You can change permissions at any time in iOS Settings › Waldo.
