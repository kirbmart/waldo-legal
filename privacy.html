Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Waldo ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use the Waldo mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account information: When you sign up, we collect your email address, display name, and (optionally) a profile photo. If you sign in with Apple, we receive a unique identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name and email.
  • Content you create: Photos, videos, captions, place names, notes, and trip guides you save or publish in the App.

Information collected automatically

  • Location: With your permission, we use your device's location to show nearby places on the map and improve discovery. You can revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Device & usage data: Basic technical information such as device model, OS version, app version, and crash logs to help us diagnose problems and improve the App.

Permissions we may request

  • Photos: To let you select photos to share or save with a place.
  • Camera: To let you take a photo or video to share or save with a place.
  • Microphone: To capture audio when you record a video in the App.
  • Location: To show nearby places and improve map discovery.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the App and its features.
  • To authenticate you and secure your account.
  • To personalize the feed and map based on the content and creators you follow.
  • To diagnose technical problems and prevent abuse.
  • To communicate with you about your account, security, or important changes to the App.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers that help us run the App, and only as needed to provide the service:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, file storage, and serverless functions.
  • Mapbox — map rendering and place search.
  • Apple — if you choose Sign in with Apple, and for App Store / TestFlight delivery.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Waldo, our users, or others.

4. Public Content

Content you publish (such as posts and public guides) may be visible to other users of the App. Do not include information in public posts that you would not want others to see.

5. Data Retention

We retain account information and the content you create for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting us at the address below.

6. Children

Waldo is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including encryption in transit and at rest where supported by our service providers. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below.

9. Beta / TestFlight

While the App is distributed via Apple's TestFlight, additional crash and diagnostic information may be collected by Apple in accordance with the Apple Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised. Material changes will be communicated within the App or by email.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Reach us at brady@goodlunch.com.
