Delete Your Account

Last updated: September 19, 2026

You can request deletion of your Waldo account and its associated data at any time. This page explains how to make the request and what happens when you do.

How to request deletion

Email brady@goodlunch.com with the subject line "Waldo account deletion request" from the email address associated with your Waldo account. We use the sending address to verify that the request comes from the account owner; if you signed up with Sign in with Apple using a private relay address, include that relay address in your message.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile (display name, handle, profile photo)
  • Posts, photos, videos, captions, tips, comments, and likes you created
  • Your saved places and follow relationships
  • Your email address and authentication records

What may be retained

  • Records we are required to keep for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes, for no longer than necessary for those purposes
  • Aggregated or de-identified data that no longer identifies you

Timing

We complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

For anything else, see our Privacy Policy or visit Support.
